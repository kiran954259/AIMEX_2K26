🧩 E-SPORTS RULES

🎮 BGMI

  1. Round 1: Erangel / Livik | Squad of 4 players | Top 8 qualify.
  2. Matches are monitored by coordinators.
  3. Round 2: Group A vs B (TDM) — One match each | ~4 winners qualify.
  4. Round 3: TDM — Winners advance to Finals.

🚫 TDM Restrictions

  1. Sliding must be OFF.
  2. Only M416 is allowed.
  3. No extra guns or pistols.
  4. No throwables allowed.
  5. No rule violations during matches.

🔥 FREE FIRE

  1. Bermuda Map | Squad of maximum 4 players.
  2. Matches are monitored by coordinators.
  3. Round 1: All teams play — Top 8 qualify.
  4. Round 2: Group A vs B (Clash Squad) — Winners qualify.
  5. Round 3: Clash Squad — Winners advance to Finals.
  6. No Gloo Wall breaking.
  7. No Gloo Pack in the last zone.
  8. No throwables allowed.
  9. No hacks, panels, or rule violations.

🧩 TUG OF WAR RULES

  1. Each team must have equal players (6–8 members).
  2. The center mark of the rope starts aligned with the ground center line.
  3. Teams must pull until the center mark crosses their winning line.
  4. The match begins only on the referee’s whistle.
  5. Proper sports shoes are required (no spikes unless permitted).
  6. Hands only — tying the rope or sitting is not allowed. Players must remain standing.
  7. No early pulling or unfair play. The first team to cross the line wins.

🧩 DODGE BALL RULES

  1. Only registered players are allowed. Proper sports attire is compulsory.
  2. Players must follow referee instructions at all times.
  3. The game is played between an Inside Team and an Outside Team.
  4. Ball hits below the knee result in OUT. Hits above the knee (head/torso) are invalid.
  5. Stepping outside the court boundary leads to elimination.
  6. Each round has a fixed time limit. Teams switch after all inside players are out.
  7. Rough play, arguments, or unfair behavior result in disqualification.
  8. The team with maximum eliminations or minimum completion time is declared the winner.
